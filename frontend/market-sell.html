Start selling on Obit Market

Submit a genuine shop and real products. Staff review your listings before they appear publicly. Buyers can shop without joining the cooperative.

  1. Apply or sign in

    New sellers start with a membership application. Already approved and active? Sign in to the member portal, then return to the marketplace. Approved members setting up access can use the password setup option. An email shopping account alone does not grant seller access.

  2. Open your seller workspace

    After member sign-in, open the seller workspace. Submit your trading name and shop description for review. An Official store badge needs staff review of brand ownership or written authorisation. Membership alone does not award the badge.

  3. Submit your actual product

    Download the product information worksheet to gather your details before completing the form.

    Enter the exact product name, category and full naira price. The current pilot accepts prices from ₦20,000 to ₦3,000,000. Describe the model, condition, included accessories and any actual warranty. Do not promise a warranty or brand relationship you cannot support.

    Add a direct HTTPS photo link for the actual item where available. Optional videos must be your own or authorised and show the listed item. Submit for review. A pending listing is not publicly available.

  4. Confirm stock on hand

    Under Your listings, enter the real quantity available and select Save stock. The screen separates stock on hand, reserved units and availability. A missing stock quantity can prevent a buyer from creating an order. Update stock after stock changes elsewhere, and resolve conflicting reservations with staff.

  5. Respond to buyer requests

    Review the products, quantities, destination and delivery preference. Confirm availability and enter a delivery fee and estimated days you can meet. The customer reviews the complete amount before accepting. Quote requests are unpaid and do not reserve goods.

  6. Follow orders and hand over goods

    Buyers pay through the company bank account shown in their order. Payment awaiting confirmation means staff still need to check the credit. A customer's message alone is not payment confirmation. For a bank-payment-confirmed order, agree handover with Obit operations. Staff record preparation, carrier and tracking or pickup readiness. Only the buyer confirms receipt.

    Seller settlement is handled separately. Agree the settlement terms with Obit before your first sale. A delivery status does not transfer seller proceeds.

  7. Help resolve problems

    Keep product and handover records. Cooperate with the team on complaints, inspection and agreed returns. Staff approval and an actual bank refund are separate steps. Returned items are not automatically put back into stock. Read the returns and refund process.
