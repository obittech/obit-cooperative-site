How Obit SafePay works

1Deal createdBuyer chooses an eligible marketplace listing.
2Payment securedFunds go to the approved third-party financial partner, not Obit Technologies or Obit Cooperative.
3Seller deliversDelivery evidence and status are recorded on the technology platform.
4Buyer confirmsBuyer confirms delivery or raises a dispute.
5SettlementThe financial partner releases or refunds funds according to the approved transaction outcome.

Platform roles

Obit Technologies Limited develops and operates the marketplace and SafePay technology infrastructure. Obit Technologies Multipurpose Cooperative Society Limited manages its cooperative community, membership and cooperative programmes. Payment custody and settlement are performed only by approved third-party financial providers under their applicable terms.
