Purchase terms

Version 2026-11-05-v1. Applies to new bank-transfer checkouts accepted under this version. Earlier purchases retain their agreed terms.

Seller and buyer eligibility

Buyers do not need cooperative membership. Sellers must be active approved cooperative members. Membership does not by itself verify ownership of a seller’s payout account. Staff must check the seller’s identity and matching payout ownership separately before any settlement.

Price, stock and delivery

The order shows products, quantities, product subtotal, delivery charge and total in Naira. No additional marketplace checkout fee is added to that total. Your bank may charge its own transfer fee. Stock is checked on order creation and checkout, with a limited reservation. The seller’s delivery estimate starts after staff confirms payment. A pickup selection is a preference until readiness is confirmed. Contact support before paying if the estimate or charges are unclear.

Payment options

This checkout is a full-payment outright purchase. It does not create an instalment plan, loan, recurring debit mandate or savings account. Any future instalment offer requires a separate approved agreement showing the deposit, full cost, due dates, delivery milestone, cancellation, refund and default terms before acceptance. No instalment deductions or default fees apply to this outright purchase.

Payment and delivery evidence

Use the company account and order reference displayed at checkout. Select I Have Paid and send the prepared WhatsApp message. Staff check the bank credit before confirming payment. Only confirm receipt after receiving the goods. Keep the tracking reference and useful delivery photographs or messages. Never send your password, NIN or BVN in a dispute message.

Problems, returns and seller settlement

Report missing, wrong, damaged or incomplete goods promptly from Orders. Aim to report visible issues within seven calendar days of receipt so evidence can be checked. This reporting target does not remove later rights for hidden faults or applicable consumer remedies. Staff review the evidence, agree any return arrangements and give a reasoned decision. An open dispute or approved refund must be resolved before seller settlement. Delivery confirmation alone does not release funds. Staff must verify seller payout ownership, bank credit and delivery evidence before a settlement decision. Automated seller release remains unavailable.

Cancellation and refunds

Unpaid orders can be cancelled without a charge. Paid cancellation and return requests require review. No blanket savings-withdrawal or instalment-cancellation fee applies to these purchases. Staff must disclose any proposed lawful deduction and its reason before agreement. Refund approval and actual bank payment are separate records, each tied to this order. Funds are not moved to another purchase or cooperative savings plan by these terms. Contact support for a progress update or to challenge a decision. Nothing here removes applicable consumer rights.
