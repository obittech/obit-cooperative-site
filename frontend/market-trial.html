Let customers
find your shop.

Try a reviewed storefront for one calendar month before deciding whether to join Obit Cooperative. Your month starts when staff first publishes your shop.

  • Shop details, goods, photos, videos and map directions.
  • Customer enquiries through your public business contact.
  • No cooperative membership required during the trial.
  • Existing membership of another cooperative does not prevent a trial.

Trial storefronts support enquiries. Obit checkout, fund holding and cooperative financial services are separate.

Free assistance to check Moniepoint loan eligibility is available on request. Eligibility and approval are determined through Moniepoint's authorised process. This is not a free loan or a promise of approval.
