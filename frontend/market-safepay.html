From order to receipt

  1. Review the product, quantity, delivery fee, estimated delivery date and seller’s return terms. Accept the seller quote to create an order.
  2. Click Pay Now. Check the company account, exact total and order reference. Use the order reference in your transfer narration.
  3. After transferring, click I Have Paid. Your order shows Payment awaiting confirmation. WhatsApp opens to +234 803 056 7787 with your order reference and amount. Tap Send to reach our team. No receipt upload is required.
  4. Staff confirms the actual bank credit. Clicking the button or sending a message does not by itself confirm payment. Once verified, your receipt appears in your shopping account.
  5. Use the same WhatsApp conversation for delivery arrangements and updates. Confirm receipt only after receiving and checking the goods.

Buyer and seller protections

  • Traceable orders: the order reference connects the product, quoted total, payment record and support conversation.
  • Payment checks: staff records a unique bank transaction reference. The same credit cannot be used to confirm two orders.
  • Member sellers: listings are restricted to active cooperative members and reviewed before publication. Membership or listing approval does not prove that every BVN, NIN or facial check is complete.
  • Delivery issues: report non-delivery, damage, a wrong item or a disputed payment to Obit with your order reference. Preserve the packaging, messages and any available photos. Our team reviews the payment and delivery evidence with both sides.
  • Refund or replacement requests: ask Obit support to review the order and applicable return terms. A request is not an automatic refund approval. This does not remove any rights available under applicable law.

What SafePay currently provides

This checkout provides company bank-transfer collection, staff verification, a payment receipt and order support. It is not a bank or an independently licensed payment gateway. A dedicated escrow account, legally agreed release conditions and automatic seller settlement have not been established by this checkout. We do not describe an ordinary company bank transfer as a guaranteed escrow deposit.

Identity checks support accountability. They do not guarantee product quality, delivery or a refund. Do not send your BVN, NIN, PIN, password or OTP through WhatsApp.
