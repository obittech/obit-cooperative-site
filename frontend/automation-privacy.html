Obit Billionaires Automation Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026. This policy applies to Obit Billionaires Automation, operated by Obinna Onuigbo for the Obit Billionaires YouTube channel.

Information accessed

With the channel owner's Google authorization, the application accesses the authorized YouTube channel ID, live stream identifiers and ingestion configuration, including the stream key, and broadcast titles, descriptions, scheduled times, visibility, stream bindings and lifecycle status. It uses OAuth access and refresh tokens to perform authorized requests. It does not request Gmail, contacts or Drive access.

How information is used

Information is used only to confirm the intended channel, identify its existing stream, avoid duplicate or overlapping broadcasts, create and bind affirmation broadcasts and complete playback. Although the YouTube permission grants broader capabilities, the current application does not use it to delete videos or access viewer comments.

Storage, protection and access

OAuth client credentials, refresh tokens and stream keys are intended to be held in the operator's private Render environment settings rather than public source files or website pages. Access tokens are held in application memory during a run. Operational logs may include broadcast IDs, playback status and error status codes. The application avoids logging credential values. Only the operator and people explicitly authorized to administer the deployment should access its credentials or logs.

Render hosts the application and processes the credentials and operational data needed to run it. Google and YouTube process authorized API requests and host published broadcasts. Network requests to these providers use HTTPS or RTMPS. Their own privacy policies also apply to their services.

Sharing and limited use

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or transferred for unrelated purposes. It is not used to train generalized AI or machine learning models. Data is shared only with service providers as needed to operate the authorized publishing workflow, with the owner's consent, or as legally required. Human access is limited to operator administration, consented support, security needs or legal obligations.

Obit Billionaires Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation and deletion

Credentials are retained while the operator has enabled the integration and removed when it is disconnected. Provider logs and backups follow the hosting provider's retention settings. Published videos and broadcasts remain on YouTube until the channel owner removes them there. Revoking authorization stops further authorized API access but does not delete previously published content.

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Contact obinnaonuigbo01@gmail.com to request disconnection, deletion of stored credentials or information about retained operational data.

Website and updates

These information pages do not ask visitors to log in and do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information. This policy will be updated if the application's data practices change.
