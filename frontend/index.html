FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What prospective members should know

Who can become a member?

Eligibility is determined by the cooperative's approved bye-laws, membership policy, KYC requirements and applicable law.

Does joining guarantee a loan or investment return?

No. Membership does not guarantee credit, investment returns or access to every programme. Each service may have separate eligibility, underwriting and regulatory requirements.

What is the Obit Network?

It is the community and economic network connecting members, businesses, opportunities, learning, hubs and shared projects under the Obit ecosystem.

How does Obit Technologies relate to the cooperative?

Obit Technologies Limited can provide approved technology, infrastructure and shared services under written agreements while the cooperative remains a separate member-governed legal institution.
