How to shop on Obit Market

Follow these steps at your own pace. Keep this page open while you shop, and return whenever you need a reminder.

What you can do today: browse approved products, save a bag, request seller quotes and review unpaid orders. Bank-transfer checkout appears in Orders when the company receiving account is enabled. A quote request does not charge you or book delivery.

  1. Find an item

    Open Shop. Tap Search products and type what you need. Use Categories to see a smaller list. If nothing appears, clear your search or try another category.

  2. Read the details and watch the item

    Tap View details beneath an item. Read the price, description and seller name. When a video is available, tap Watch product video. Videos are supplied by sellers and reviewed before publication. If the video cannot load, read the description and ask the seller for more details in your quote request.

  3. Add to your bag

    Tap Add to bag. Open Bag at the bottom of the screen. Change Quantity or tap Remove if you no longer want an item. Your bag is a selection, not a completed purchase.

  4. Sign in to your shopping account

    Tap Account. Enter your email and tap Send sign-in code. Check your inbox and spam folder. Enter the six-digit code, then tap Verify and continue. Keep the code private. Shopping does not require cooperative membership.

  5. Save your delivery details

    In your account, tap Delivery. Enter the recipient’s name, phone number, street address, town and state. Tap Save delivery details. Choose home delivery for a seller quote, or select a verified pickup location when one is available. Saving an address does not book a courier.

  6. Ask the seller for a quote

    Return to Bag in your account. Check the items, quantities, prices and delivery preference. Tap Request seller quotes. Open Requests to follow the response. Each seller confirms the delivery fee and estimated delivery days separately.

  7. Review the quote and follow your order

    Check the full amount and delivery terms before accepting a quote. Accepting creates an unpaid order with a short stock reservation. Open Orders to see its current status. When Pay Now is available, open it to see the company bank details, exact amount and order reference. Transfer the money, then click I Have Paid. WhatsApp opens to our team with the order details. No receipt upload is needed. Your payment remains awaiting confirmation until staff checks the bank credit. Our team follows up through delivery. Never treat a quote or an unpaid order as proof of payment.

SafePay instructions and your protections

Need a quick answer?

Do I pay to create a shopping account?

No. Shopping accounts are free. Sellers must meet the cooperative’s membership and listing requirements.

Why is no pickup location listed?

Only locations reviewed and activated by staff appear. Choose a home-delivery quote or check again later.

Why can I not see any products?

The store shows approved, active listings. Clear your filters. If none remain, there may be no approved items available yet.

Can I check out and pay now?

Open Orders and look for Pay Now. When enabled, it shows the company bank details. If it is unavailable, do not transfer money. The I Have Paid button reports a transfer and opens WhatsApp. Staff must confirm the actual bank credit before a receipt is issued.
