## Proposed initial service targets

These are proposed Obit operating commitments, not claims about statutory minimum rights. Applicable rights and product warranties must remain available even where an internal reporting target is missed.

| Issue | Proposed handling | Capacity/decision still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Seller onboarding packet

Collect the seller's existing active member reference, trading name, authorised contact, actual stock owner, product title/model, condition, included items, full price, quantity physically on hand, actual product photo/video rights, warranty terms and dispatch availability. Do not collect BVN/NIN in this worksheet or chat.

Use frontend/assets/seller-product-worksheet.csv to gather this information. It is a preparation worksheet, not an automated importer. Enter each confirmed item through the existing seller workspace. Keep brand-authorisation documents in the approved staff review process. Official-store status and listing approval are separate decisions.

## Pickup and delivery setup

For the first pilot choose one genuinely available delivery arrangement. A manually arranged local handover is sufficient if both parties agree, even without a courier API. Capture: responsible person, contact, origin/destination, service area, quoted fee, estimated date, tracking/handover reference, failed-delivery handling and returns arrangement. Do not label it an automatic courier booking.

A pickup location additionally needs the exact public address, directions, opening hours, operator consent, named handover person, secure storage, package identification and a buyer-recipient check. A staff member creates a DRAFT in Admin, checks actual readiness, and explicitly activates it. Do not travel to or advertise a draft location as ready.

No courier account is created, contract accepted, provider contacted or dispatch booked by this proposal. Courier API integration should follow a chosen provider's verified contract and credentials.

## Real order pilot, with actual evidence

1. Identify one real product within the current ₦20,000–₦3,000,000 listing range, active seller, physical stock and agreed buyer. Fix a total spending limit and who funds the purchase. Do not buy solely from an online research price or treat a quote as stock ownership.
2. Seller submits the product and correct stock. Staff independently reviews and approves it. Verify it appears in the live catalogue and that the seller has permission for product media.
3. Buyer verifies their own shopping email and saves delivery details. Use actual data only with the person's consent. Do not include personal details in public screenshots or the eventual tutorial.
4. Buyer requests a quote. Seller confirms availability, total delivery fee and timing. Buyer accepts the reviewed quote.
5. Buyer opens Pay Now, checks the official company account and total, and personally makes the real bank transfer. No agent-created payment, fake receipt or false bank confirmation is allowed.
6. Buyer clicks I Have Paid. Confirm the record remains awaiting verification and the WhatsApp message includes the right order reference and amount.
7. Authorised staff checks the actual credit in the bank, records its unique reference and confirms payment. Check one receipt and a single stock deduction, including on refresh/retry.
8. Staff prepares and records an actual carrier/handover. For pickup, use only a verified active collection point. Buyer confirms receipt only after receiving the real goods.
9. Record outcome, remaining problems and seller settlement separately. A manual status update alone does not prove delivery or payout.
10. Exercise the return/refund branch only for an agreed genuine return or separately authorised pilot return. Staff inspect returned goods, decide an amount, make the actual bank refund outside the marketplace, verify the debit/recipient and record it once. Buyer checks actual bank receipt. Do not refund a settled purchase without the parties' agreement.
