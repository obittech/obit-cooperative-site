# Seller trial

Non-members use existing email-code shopping sign-in. One trial per verified email and normalised Nigerian business phone. No member record or financial access is created.

Staff review the shop, location, media permissions and up to 20 goods. First publication starts one calendar month, clamped at month end. Edits require review and never reset dates. Public requests exclude expired, suspended and unapproved trials. Owners retain their private data. Trial storefronts support enquiries only, without checkout or fund holding.

Staff can link an already active member with the same verified email. This does not approve membership or payments. Ongoing publication requires active membership and an approved profile.

Admin: Marketplace > Seller trial reviews. Every decision requires a verification note. Other cooperative membership and loan interest remain private. Moniepoint wording offers free assistance to check eligibility without promising a loan. No provider API or automatic follow-up is connected.

Registration requires existing email delivery configuration. Do not send real trader test codes during release checks.
